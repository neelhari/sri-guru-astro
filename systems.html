VEDIC TRADITIONS

Our Astrology Systems

We combine classical Vedic, Nadi, KP Astrology, and Numerology to give you clear guidance and accurate remedies.

SYSTEM 01

Classical Vedic Astrology (Parashari)

The foundation of Indian astrology based on Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra.

Vedic astrology analyzes your birth chart through 12 houses, 9 planets, and 27 Nakshatras. It helps you understand your life path, career, marriage, health, and current planetary periods (Dasha).

SYSTEM 02

Bhrigu Nandi Nadi

An ancient Nadi method for clear and direct life predictions.

Bhrigu Nandi Nadi analyzes planetary combinations to understand your past karma, current life situations, and future possibilities quickly and accurately.

SYSTEM 03

KP System (Krishnamurti Padhdhati)

A modern, precise system for exact timing of life events.

Developed by Prof. K.S. Krishnamurti, the KP system uses Nakshatra sub-divisions to accurately predict the timing of key events like marriage, jobs, business growth, and property matters.

SYSTEM 04

Numerology

Understanding the power of numbers and name vibrations.

Numerology analyzes your birth date and name spelling. It is widely used for choosing auspicious baby names, business name corrections, and selecting lucky dates.
