METHODOLOGIES OF PRECISION

Our Astrology Systems

Integrating four time-tested classical traditions to deliver multi-dimensional accuracy for your chart.

SYSTEM 01

Classical Vedic Astrology (Parashari)

The foundational pillar of Sanatana astrology rooted in the Brihat Parasara Hora Shastra.

Vedic astrology maps the 12 houses (Bhavas), 9 planets (Navagrahas), and 27 lunar mansions (Nakshatras) at the exact moment of your birth. Through the Vimshottari Dasha system, it unveils the master timeline of your life's unfolding karma, strengths, and spiritual evolution.

SYSTEM 02

Bhrigu Nandi Nadi

Ancient Nadi discipline deciphering planetary linkages and karmic destiny trails.

Bhrigu Nandi Nadi provides swift, deep insights by analyzing directional groupings and planetary combinations. It reveals the purpose behind your current life circumstances, relationships, and major turning points without requiring cumbersome calculations.

SYSTEM 03

KP System (Krishnamurti Padhdhati)

Precision science using stellar sub-lords for pin-pointed event predictions.

Developed by the late Prof. K.S. Krishnamurti, the KP system divides Nakshatras into precise sub-divisions ruled by 'Sub-Lords'. This eliminates ambiguity in timing major events like marriage dates, job offers, court matters, and property transactions.

SYSTEM 04

Numerology

The cosmic vibrational frequency of birth dates and name vibrations.

Numerology decodes the resonance between your birth day number, destiny number, and name spelling. We utilize numerological harmony for newborn baby naming, business name corrections, and selecting auspicious dates.
